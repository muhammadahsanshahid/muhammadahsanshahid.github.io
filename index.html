About Me

Hi, I'm Muhammad Ahsan Shahid.

I'm a Digital Marketing, AI Solutions and Web Development professional based in Lahore, Pakistan. I enjoy the point where technology meets growth: building a website is only half the job, the other half is making sure the right people find it, trust it and buy from it.

I've built and managed live business websites and Shopify stores, developed custom software like a Point of Sale system and a University Online Voting System, and run campaigns for local businesses and daily rental properties. I also love teaching people how to get real results from AI tools like Gemini, ChatGPT and Claude.
